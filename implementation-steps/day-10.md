# Day 10

Task: Case V 3D TFM run on the ORNL/UTK cold-mockup geometry (S1 + S2).

Build a 3D cut-cell / STL template of the vessel (`src/make_stl.py` surface of revolution: 60° cone + straight column, 4 mm constant-diameter orifice at the apex). Cell size ≤ 10·d_p = 5 mm in the particle zone; at least 4 cells across the 4 mm inlet (so the near-axis cell size is ≤ 1 mm; grade if needed). Closures same as Case V_2d (Syamlal–O'Brien, Schaeffer with "modified sigmoidal" blending, PDE granular energy, phi = 15°, ep_star = 0.42). Operating point 30 m/s inlet jet, air at ambient.

Validation targets (digitized CSVs under `data/digitized/`, each file labeled as digitized with the figure number):
- Centerline axial gas velocity vs height, S1 Fig. 1.
- Inlet-pressure pulsation frequency: S1 Fig. 2 (~10–11 Hz, ORNL 2-inch coater) and S2 (17–25 Hz over U/U_ms = 1.3–2.3, UTK mockup).

Deliverables:

- `build_V_3d_params()` in `src/make_case.py`.
- Run driver + post-processor (frames, ΔP, PSD, centerline v_g vs S1 Fig. 1 overlay).
- At least ≈ 5 s of simulated time after startup transient; wall-time estimate from Day 3 CFL tool before launch.
- `results/fig_d10_V_3d_centerline_vg.png`, `results/fig_d10_V_3d_psd.png`.
