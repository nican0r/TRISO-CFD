# Day 9

Run V_2d at an inlet velocity of 1.2× U_ms (Mathur–Gishler) for ≥ 5 s of simulated time. Use Gidaspow or Syamlal–O'Brien drag (record which) and e = 0.9. Deliverables: an animation or frame sequence of solids fraction, the ΔP time series, and a check of the max CFL per src/cfl_check.py. If the run fails or doesn't spout: first diagnose (dt too large? orifice under-resolved? initial bed too tall? inlet velocity below spouting?), write the diagnosis in notes/day09_debug.md, then make one change at a time and log each run. 

Done when: a visually clear spout, fountain and annulus exist and persist for several seconds.
